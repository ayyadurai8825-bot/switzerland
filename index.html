About Switzerland

Switzerland is one of the most beautiful countries in Europe. It is famous for the Swiss Alps, snow-covered mountains, crystal-clear lakes, green valleys, chocolates, cheese, luxury watches, and peaceful cities. Every year millions of tourists visit Switzerland to enjoy its breathtaking natural beauty and outdoor adventures.

Beautiful Places

Zurich

Zurich is Switzerland's largest city known for shopping, culture, museums and beautiful lakes.

Interlaken

Interlaken is surrounded by mountains and lakes. It is famous for adventure sports and scenic beauty.

Matterhorn

Matterhorn is one of the most famous mountains in the world and a symbol of Switzerland.
