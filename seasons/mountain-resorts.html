Choose a resort for your trip

Swiss mountain resorts offer access to alpine scenery and outdoor activities. Choose a destination based on the season, activities you enjoy and the type of trip you want, from mountain walks to winter sports.

Five mountain places to visit

Zermatt

A car-free mountain village with views of the Matterhorn and access to alpine walks and winter sports.

Grindelwald

A village in the Jungfrau region, surrounded by dramatic peaks and routes for hiking and mountain excursions.

St. Moritz

An alpine resort in the Engadine known for mountain scenery and outdoor activities throughout the year.

Verbier

A Valais resort with access to mountain trails and a large winter-sports area.

Davos

A high-altitude town in Graubünden offering mountain paths, scenic views and seasonal winter activities.

Plan ahead

  • Check local weather, trail conditions and lift operations before setting out.
  • Confirm resort opening dates and transport schedules for your travel dates.
  • Pack suitable layers and footwear for changing mountain conditions.
