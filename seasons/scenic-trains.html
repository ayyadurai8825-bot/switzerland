Make the train journey part of your trip

Switzerland's rail network connects cities, lakeside towns and mountain destinations. Scenic routes let you enjoy the views along the way, while regular trains can make it easy to plan journeys between places.

Before you travel

  • Check the timetable and any required seat reservations before departure.
  • Allow extra time when changing trains, especially when carrying luggage.
  • Check ticket and pass conditions for the route you plan to take.
