Enjoy chocolate in Switzerland

Swiss chocolate is a popular treat to sample during your visit. Try different styles from local shops and chocolatiers, or look for a chocolate-making experience to learn how cocoa is turned into finished chocolate.

Helpful tips

  • Check opening hours and tour availability before visiting a chocolate attraction.
  • Ask about ingredients if you have allergies or dietary requirements.
  • Keep chocolate cool and out of direct sunlight while travelling.
