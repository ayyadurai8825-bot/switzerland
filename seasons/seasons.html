Two seasons, two unforgettable trips

Choose your season based on the landscapes and activities you want most.

When should you visit?

Summer generally brings longer days and open hiking routes. Winter brings snow in many mountain areas and a lively ski season. Conditions vary by altitude and year, so check local forecasts, lift operations and trail reports before travelling.

Summer: roughly June to September

Summer is a great fit for hiking, lake outings, cycling and seeing alpine meadows. Mountain weather can change quickly, and some high trails may still have snow early in the season.

Good choices

  • Interlaken and the Jungfrau region: mountain views, villages and marked walks.
  • Lake Lucerne: boat trips, lakeside towns and mountain excursions.
  • Zermatt: Matterhorn viewpoints and high-alpine walks when routes are open.

Pack: comfortable walking shoes, layers, rain protection, sun protection and a refillable water bottle.

Winter: roughly December to March

Winter is best for skiing, snowboarding, sledging and snowy village scenery. Snow reliability differs between resorts and elevations; check opening dates and current snow reports before booking activities.

Good choices

  • Zermatt: a mountain-resort base with views of the Matterhorn.
  • Jungfrau region: winter sports and mountain villages around Grindelwald and Wengen.
  • Lucerne: a city break with easy access to nearby mountain excursions, weather permitting.

Pack: a warm waterproof coat, insulated layers, gloves, a hat and footwear with good grip.
