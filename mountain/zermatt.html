Discover Zermatt

Zermatt is a car-free alpine village known for views of the Matterhorn. Visitors can explore the village, take mountain excursions, and enjoy walking routes or winter activities when conditions allow.

Plan your visit

  • Check weather, trail conditions and mountain transport schedules before setting out.
  • Wear layers and suitable footwear for changing alpine conditions.
  • Review seasonal opening times and reserve activities in advance when needed.
Visit the official Zermatt website

Why visit Zermatt?

From glacier views and mountain railways to village walks and relaxed alpine dining, Zermatt offers a classic Swiss mountain experience for travelers looking for scenery, fresh air, and memorable outdoor adventures.
