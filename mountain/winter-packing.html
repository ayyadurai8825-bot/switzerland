Carry warm clothes for winter travel

Switzerland can feel much colder than it looks, especially in mountain regions or at higher altitude. A good winter packing list helps you stay comfortable and safe while exploring towns, trails and ski areas.

Essentials to pack

  • Warm waterproof coat or insulated winter jacket
  • Thermal layers and fleece or down mid-layers
  • Gloves, warm hat and neck scarf
  • Waterproof boots with good grip
  • Thermal socks and moisture-wicking base layers
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen for bright alpine days

Stay prepared outdoors

  • Check the weather forecast before leaving your hotel
  • Pack layers so you can adjust to indoor and outdoor temperatures
  • Bring a refillable water bottle and a small snack for mountain trips
  • Use traction-friendly footwear on icy paths and sidewalks
  • Plan extra time for slow travel in snow or winter conditions

Quick winter checklist

If you are visiting mountain areas, always bring warm layers, proper footwear and weatherproof outerwear. Even a short walk can feel much colder once the wind picks up.
